One-line elevator pitch a city pigeon gives investors for its startup: after ten years decorating every statue in the city for free, it will charge the city to undo its own art. The joke is the twist — the pigeon is the mess, and now it sells the cleanup.

## How it works

`main.py` builds the pitch from three parts, keeps two rejected drafts beside it with the reason each one lost, and checks the winner before writing it: exactly one line, 1–200 characters, no trailing newline. The checked line goes to `/output/entry.txt`.

```sh
python3 main.py
```
